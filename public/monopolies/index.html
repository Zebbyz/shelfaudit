Shelf Audit / Who controls NZ

Market concentration

Your money isn't yours for long.

A few companies control most of what you buy. Every slice below is sourced.

Number 1: Liquor

Liquorland is owned by Foodstuffs, the Pak'nSave and New World co-op

The same co-ops that run Pak'nSave, New World and Four Square also own the Liquorland bottle store chain. Foodstuffs also owns the liquor wholesalers Gilmours and Trents, which are set to dominate wholesale supply once Metcash exits in December 2026.

20%of all New Zealand liquor is sold through Foodstuffs supermarkets
over 60%of beer, wine and cider is sold by the two supermarket groups
2Foodstuffs chains selling liquor: supermarkets and Liquorland

All liquor in New Zealand

  • Foodstuffs supermarkets 20%
  • Everyone else, including Liquorland 80%

Source: The Post, Foodstuffs to dominate liquor wholesaling after Aussie-owned Metcash withdraws (25 September 2026). Liquorland bottle store sales are on top of this 20%.

Beer, wine and cider (no spirits)

  • Foodstuffs and Woolworths supermarkets over 60%
  • Everyone else under 40%

Source: NZ Herald, Power List: New Zealand's top liquor retailers (1 September 2023).

No public source gives Liquorland's own share of bottle store sales, so we have not drawn one.

The rest of the list

Supermarkets

3 companies control 82% of grocery sales

  • Woolworths 27%
  • Pak'nSave (Foodstuffs) 25%
  • New World (Foodstuffs) 22%
  • Other Foodstuffs and Woolworths banners 8%
  • Everyone else 18%

Source: Commerce Commission, Annual Grocery Report 2025 (published 2 June 2026). Shares by banner. The three regulated grocery retailers are Woolworths NZ, Foodstuffs North Island and Foodstuffs South Island.

Domestic airlines

1 airline flies 86% of the domestic market

  • Air New Zealand 86%
  • Jetstar 13%
  • Everyone else 1%

Source: Consumer NZ, How do we fix our domestic aviation market? (22 August 2024).

Mobile phones

3 networks hold 97.5% of mobile connections

  • Spark 38%
  • One NZ 38%
  • 2degrees 22%
  • Everyone else 2%

Source: Commerce Commission, 2025 Telecommunications Monitoring Report (29 June 2026). MVNOs, the resellers on these networks, are under 3.2%. Rounded shares.

Fuel

3 majors supply over 90% of retail fuel

  • Z, BP and Mobil over 90%
  • Everyone else, including Gull under 10%

Source: Commerce Commission, Retail fuel market study final report (5 December 2019). ComCom did not publish a split between the three majors.

Banking

4 Australian-owned banks hold about 85 to 90% of banking assets

  • ANZ about 30%
  • ASB 18 to 20%
  • BNZ 18 to 20%
  • Westpac 18 to 20%
  • Everyone else, including Kiwibank about 10 to 15%

Source: Commerce Commission, Personal banking services market study final report (20 August 2024). Shares of total assets, approximate as given by ComCom.

Electricity

4 retailers supply 86.5% of homes and businesses

  • Mercury 25.2%
  • Genesis 22.0%
  • Contact 20.3%
  • Meridian 18.9%
  • Pulse 3.4%
  • Nova 3.4%
  • Everyone else 6.7%

Source: Electricity Authority, EMI market share snapshot, connections (ICPs) by parent company at 30 September 2026.

Building supplies

3 merchants make over 80% of key building supply sales

  • Three largest merchants (PlaceMakers is the biggest) over 80%
  • Everyone else under 20%

Source: Commerce Commission, Residential building supplies market study final report (6 December 2022). ComCom did not publish each merchant's share.

Home insurance

2 groups wrote about 71% of home insurance

  • IAG (AMI, State, NZI and others) 46%
  • Suncorp (Vero, AA Insurance) 25%
  • Tower 5%
  • Everyone else 24%

Source: Commerce Commission figures from the Vero and Tower merger decision, as reported by interest.co.nz (8 August 2017). Older data, the latest public split.

Pharmacy

2 chains lead the pharmacy market

Unichem and Life Pharmacy (Green Cross Health network): 328 stores
Chemist Warehouse: 70 stores

Store counts, not market share. No independent share figure is published. Euromonitor ranks Green Cross Health first and Chemist Warehouse second. Chemist Warehouse claims 66% of retail pharmacy sales in its own briefing to a minister; that claim is not independently verified, so it is not charted.

Sources: Green Cross Health full year result, NZX (29 May 2025), stores at 31 March 2025; Inside Retail NZ (13 April 2026), stores at end of 2025; Euromonitor, Consumer Health in New Zealand.

Bread

2 bakers dominate packaged bread

Goodman Fielder
George Weston Foods

Company count, not market share. No current public share figures.

Source: Commerce Commission Decision 599, Goodman Fielder and River Mill Bakeries.

Shares are rounded and come from the latest public figure we could find, so years differ by market. Electronics retail was left out because no reliable published share exists. Spotted a newer figure? Tell us and we will update it.
