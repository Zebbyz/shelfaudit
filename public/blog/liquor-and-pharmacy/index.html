It's not just groceries: liquor and pharmacy

28 Oct 2026 · Shelf Audit

Duopoly Liquor Pharmacy

Foodstuffs supermarkets sell 20% of all NZ liquor and it owns Liquorland. Two chains lead pharmacy.

  • 20% of all NZ liquor is sold through Foodstuffs supermarkets, and Foodstuffs also owns Liquorland and the wholesalers Gilmours and Trents.[1]
  • The two supermarket groups sell over 60% of beer, wine and cider.[2]
  • Pharmacy: the Green Cross Health network (Unichem and Life Pharmacy) had 328 stores at 31 March 2025, and Chemist Warehouse about 70 at the end of 2025.[3][4]
