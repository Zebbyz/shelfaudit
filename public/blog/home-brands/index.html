Home brands: the supermarket owns the shelf and the label

7 Nov 2026 · Shelf Audit

Home brands Suppliers

Private label is about 14.5% of packaged supermarket sales. The retailer sets the price of its own brand and its rivals'.

  • Private label (Pams, Woolworths brand and others) was about 14.5% of pre-packaged supermarket sales, per Circana data cited by the Commerce Commission.[1]
  • Suppliers often make both their own brand and the supermarket's private label, chosen by tender.[1][2]
  • The Commission says its monitoring found no evidence of preferential treatment for private label, but that monitoring covered only a small number of range reviews.[1]

Opinion: When one company owns the shelf, sets the shelf price and owns one of the brands on it, shoppers deserve to see who made what. Shelf Audit is mapping brand owners next.
