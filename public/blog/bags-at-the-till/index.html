Bags: from free to for sale

3 Nov 2026 · Shelf Audit

Fees Packaging

Since the 2019 plastic bag ban, supermarkets sell paper and reusable bags. Nobody has published what they make on them.

  • Single-use plastic shopping bags were banned from 1 July 2019.[1]
  • Countdown (now Woolworths) charged 20c for paper bags and sold a $1 reusable bag when the ban began.[2]
  • No supermarket has published its margin on bag sales. We could not find one.

Opinion: The ban was right. But a bag line you pay for, at a self-checkout you run, with a bagging shelf too small to use, adds up.

Sources

  1. Ministry for the Environment: Single-use plastic shopping bag ban, facts for businesses, 2019
  2. Supermarket News: Countdown introduces paper bags, 2019
