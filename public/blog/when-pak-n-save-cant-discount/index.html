When a Pak'nSave is told not to discount

3 Nov 2026 · Shelf Audit

Commerce Commission Court Pricing

The Commission is suing Foodstuffs South Island, alleging it restricted its own Pak'nSaves from cutting prices. Foodstuffs NI faces a separate cartel case.

In August 2026 the Commerce Commission announced civil proceedings against Foodstuffs South Island. It alleges the co-op restricted independently owned Pak'nSave stores from discounting selected products without approval, under its "Super Deal" and "Every Day Low Price" rules, which the Commission says is resale price maintenance under section 37 of the Commerce Act.[1]

The Commission also has proceedings against Foodstuffs North Island and liquor wholesaler Gilmours alleging cartel conduct.[2]

Both cases are allegations before the courts. Nothing has been proven.

Opinion: If the allegation is right, a shop wanted to charge you less and was not allowed to. Watch this case.
