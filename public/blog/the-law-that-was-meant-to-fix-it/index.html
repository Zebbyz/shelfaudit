The law that was meant to fix it

9 Nov 2026 · Shelf Audit

Law Commerce Commission Duopoly

The Grocery Industry Competition Act 2023 created a Grocery Commissioner. Three annual reports later, the 82% has not moved.

  • The Grocery Industry Competition Act 2023 came into force on 10 July 2023. It created a Grocery Commissioner inside the Commerce Commission and a wholesale regime requiring the majors to consider supply requests from rivals.[1][2]
  • The 2025 Annual Grocery Report says "little observable change" in core competition metrics, with the majors on 82%.[3]
  • The Commission has said cost rises should not be "used as an opportunity to widen margins", and prices should come down quickly when costs fall.[4]

Opinion: Watchdogs need data. That is why Shelf Audit prices every store.
