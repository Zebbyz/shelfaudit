The price of not going in: delivery fees

11 Nov 2026 · Shelf Audit

Online Fees

Woolworths lifted its Delivery Saver fee and the free delivery threshold from $80 to $100 in October 2025.

  • From 1 October 2025, Woolworths' Delivery Saver went from $23 to $25.99 a month, and from $118 to $132.99 for six months.[1]
  • The minimum order for free delivery rose from $80 to $100.[1]
  • Online is now 8% of the major retailers' sales, and click and collect outsells delivery, the Commission reports.[2]

Opinion: A higher threshold nudges a bigger basket. Price your delivery fee into your shop.

Sources

  1. RNZ: Woolworths increases subscription fee, minimum spend for free delivery, 2025
  2. Commerce Commission: Annual Grocery Report 2025, 2 June 2026
