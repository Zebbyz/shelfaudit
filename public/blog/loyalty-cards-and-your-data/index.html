Loyalty cards: you pay with your data

6 Nov 2026 · Shelf Audit

Loyalty Data

Everyday Rewards and Clubcard collect detailed purchase data. Consumer NZ warns member pricing can leave non-members paying more.

Member prices look like a gift. Consumer NZ says the trade is your data.[1]

  • Woolworths' Everyday Rewards and New World's Clubcard collect detailed purchase and behaviour data, which can be shared with partners and used for targeted ads, Consumer NZ reports.[1]
  • Consumer NZ warns member-only pricing can disadvantage shoppers without a card, an app or internet access.[2]
  • It has not found individualised pricing in NZ, but says the data could make it possible.[3]

What the regulator said

The 2022 market study found the major chains' promotions, price labelling and loyalty schemes make it harder to compare prices over time and between stores.[4]
