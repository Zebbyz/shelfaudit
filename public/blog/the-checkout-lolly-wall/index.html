The checkout lolly wall

15 Nov 2026 · Shelf Audit

Store layout Health

A University of Auckland study of 204 NZ supermarkets found only 15% of checkouts were free of junk food.

The queue is where you are tired, waiting, and often with kids.

  • A national study of 204 NZ supermarkets found on average only 15% of checkout displays were free of junk food and confectionery.[1][2]
  • Only 26.5% of stores had at least 20% of checkouts junk-food free.[2]

Opinion: Placement is a choice. A lolly-free lane costs a store nothing but a sale you did not plan.

Sources

  1. European Journal of Clinical Nutrition: Towards healthier supermarkets, a national study in New Zealand, 2018
  2. University of Auckland: Great opportunity for supermarkets to encourage healthy food choices, 19 January 2018
