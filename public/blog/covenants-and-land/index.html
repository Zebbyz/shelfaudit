190 land deals that kept rivals out

24 Oct 2026 · Shelf Audit

Duopoly Land Law

The Commission found 190 covenants and exclusivity clauses blocking competitor sites. 150 lasted more than 20 years. Parliament banned them.

A supermarket needs a site. In 2022 the Commerce Commission found the two major groups had used covenants and lease clauses to stop sites going to competitors.[1][2]

  • More than 190 restrictive and exclusivity covenants were identified.[2]
  • 150 of them restricted sites for more than 20 years.[3]
  • The Commission named a lack of suitable sites as one of the biggest barriers for new competitors, and recommended monitoring land banking.[1]

The fix

The Commerce (Grocery Sector Covenants) Amendment Act 2022 makes these grocery covenants prohibited and unenforceable.[4]
