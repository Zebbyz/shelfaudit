Same chain, same product, different price

1 Nov 2026 · Shelf Audit

Shelf Audit data Pricing

Our 1 November 2026 capture: 50.5% of 426 NZ supermarkets price above the national median. All 125 New World stores do.

Shelf Audit compares the same products across every store we can price. Here is what the capture of 1 November 2026 shows.[1]

  • 426 priced supermarkets nationwide.
  • 215 stores (50.5%) price above the national median. 211 (49.5%) are at or below it.
  • The 215 above the median are on average 2.5% dearer, ranging from 0.1% to 6.6% over.
  • The 211 at or below average 3.0% cheaper.
  • All 125 New World stores we priced sit above the median, with price indexes from 101.4 to 106.6.

What that means

Two stores with the same logo can charge different prices for the same item. Which store you walk into changes your bill.

Opinion: Higher prices are a choice. Look up your nearest stores on the Shelf Audit watchdog.
