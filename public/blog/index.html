    Specials that were not special: the court cases

    Two Pak'nSaves have been convicted and fined $580,500. Woolworths faces criminal charges it has pleaded not guilty to.

    Our 1 November 2026 capture: 50.5% of 426 NZ supermarkets price above the national median. All 125 New World stores do.

    Shrinkflation: same price, less in the pack

    Consumer NZ has tracked packs that shrank while the price stayed put, from rice crackers to chocolate boxes.

    Loyalty cards: you pay with your data

    Everyday Rewards and Clubcard collect detailed purchase data. Consumer NZ warns member pricing can leave non-members paying more.

    Home brands · 3 Nov 2026
    Home brands: the supermarket owns the shelf and the label

    Private label is about 14.5% of packaged supermarket sales. The retailer sets the price of its own brand and its rivals'.

    How suppliers are treated, and the code meant to fix it

    Rebates and payments from suppliers hit about $6 billion in FY25. Both big groups have had Grocery Supply Code warnings.

    Duopoly · 3 Nov 2026
    190 land deals that kept rivals out

    The Commission found 190 covenants and exclusivity clauses blocking competitor sites. 150 lasted more than 20 years. Parliament banned them.

    When a Pak'nSave is told not to discount

    The Commission is suing Foodstuffs South Island, alleging it restricted its own Pak'nSaves from cutting prices. Foodstuffs NI faces a separate cartel case.

    Online · 3 Nov 2026
    The price of not going in: delivery fees

    Woolworths lifted its Delivery Saver fee and the free delivery threshold from $80 to $100 in October 2025.

    Fees · 3 Nov 2026

    Since the 2019 plastic bag ban, supermarkets sell paper and reusable bags. Nobody has published what they make on them.

    The checkout lolly wall

    Duopoly · 3 Nov 2026
    It's not just groceries: liquor and pharmacy

    Foodstuffs supermarkets sell 20% of all NZ liquor and it owns Liquorland. Two chains lead pharmacy.

    Law · 3 Nov 2026
    The law that was meant to fix it

    The Grocery Industry Competition Act 2023 created a Grocery Commissioner. Three annual reports later, the 82% has not moved.
