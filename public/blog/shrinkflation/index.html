Shrinkflation: same price, less in the pack

3 Nov 2026 · Shelf Audit

Shrinkflation Pricing

Consumer NZ has tracked packs that shrank while the price stayed put, from rice crackers to chocolate boxes.

The price tag is the same. The pack is smaller. Consumer NZ has documented examples on NZ shelves.[1][2]

  • Snickers LCMs: 132g down to 100g.[1]
  • Peckish rice crackers: 100g down to 90g.[1]
  • Cadbury Favourites boxes: 373g to 340g, and 570g to 520g.[3]

Shrinkflation is usually a manufacturer's decision, not the supermarket's. But it is the supermarket shelf where it lands.

Your defence: the unit price

Since 31 August 2024, large NZ grocery stores must show a unit price (per kg, per litre) on regulated products. It must be clear and at least 25% of the size of the main price.[4] Compare per kg, not per pack.
