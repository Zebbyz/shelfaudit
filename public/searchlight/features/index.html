Features

  • Upload any buy listPaste a list, upload a CSV, or a saved online cart from any NZ supermarket site.
  • Cheapest local storePrices your whole list at every store near you from nightly Shelf Audit data.
  • We only earn when you saveShelf Audit clips 10% of the dollars saved. Save $66.70, pay $6.67. No saving, no fee.
  • Savings in dollarsShows exactly how much your group saves per shop and per term or year.
  • Distance shownEvery store comes with the km from your base so you know if the drive is worth it.
  • Line by line compareSee every item side by side, and which lines carry the biggest markups.
  • Split shop (soon)When two stores together beat one, Searchlight tells you what to buy where.
  • Bulk pooling (soon)Pool orders across several schools, clubs or businesses for bigger buys.
  • Board and committee reportExport a CSV or PDF savings brief for your board, PTA, club committee or accounts team.
  • Works for businessesCafes, offices, daycares, rest homes and trades crews buying staples every week.

Submit back to Searchlight

Tick one optional box after a compare and Shelf Audit sends the dearer supermarket a short report: when it happened, that a local school, business or club shopped elsewhere after comparing their store, both totals, and the dollars saved. The Commerce Commission gets a copy.

Example: on Wednesday at 3:45 pm a local business spent $388.27 at PAK'nSAVE Papakura after FreshChoice Papakura came back at $454.97 for the same list. They saved $66.70.
